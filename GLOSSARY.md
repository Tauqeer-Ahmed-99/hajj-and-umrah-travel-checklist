# NMT Travel

The language for organizing family participation and preparation for Hajj and Umrah trips.

## Language

**Trip**:
A planned Hajj or Umrah journey with its travelers and preparation for outbound and return travel. A trip contains one or more family groups.
_Avoid_: Using family group to mean the entire trip.

**Family group**:
A group of travelers within a trip whose preparation is organized together. The family's checklists, bags, and guides are accessible within that family by default unless explicitly shared with the trip.
_Avoid_: Family account, shared login.

**Traveler**:
A person recorded as participating in a trip, whether or not they have their own account. A member can manage a traveler profile for a child or elder.
_Avoid_: User or member when referring only to a person on the travel roster.

**Trip member**:
A person with an account who has access to a specific trip. Membership is separate from the traveler profiles that person helps manage.
_Avoid_: Traveler when referring specifically to account access.

**Trip organizer**:
A trip member responsible for trip details and trip invitations. Organizing a trip does not automatically grant access to another family's private content.
_Avoid_: Family coordinator when referring to trip administration.

**Family coordinator**:
A member responsible for a family's membership, access grants, and content sharing. Each family has at least one coordinator, whose authority is separate from organizing the trip.
_Avoid_: Trip organizer when referring to family access decisions.

**Family content**:
Checklist entries, bags, or guides belonging to one family group and available within that family by default. A trip organizer from another family also needs explicit family permission to access this content.
_Avoid_: Treating trip membership as access to every family's content.

**Trip-shared content**:
Content explicitly shared with trip members, editable by members with edit access, and with visibility controlled by the owning family's coordinators. This audience excludes people who have merely signed up for the app.
_Avoid_: Public content, public trip data.

**Trip-shared bag**:
A bag whose contents are visible to trip members and which contains only explicitly trip-shared items. Putting an item in this bag does not itself authorize sharing that item.
_Avoid_: Public bag, implicit item sharing.

**Username**:
The globally unique, case-insensitive handle identifying a member's account. It is distinct from the member's first and last names used for display, and from their private email address.
_Avoid_: Display name, family name, email address.

**Invitation**:
A recipient-specific offer of access addressed to a verified email or an existing username. Trip admission requires organizer approval, and family access requires that family's coordinator approval.
_Avoid_: Public join pass, unrestricted invite link.

**Guide**:
A document, image, text note, or link shared with a family or trip to help with preparation and travel. Uploaded documents and images open in the app by default.
_Avoid_: Public file when referring to a family-restricted guide.

**Offline guide copy**:
A guide explicitly saved inside the app for later viewing without a connection. Opening a guide and its temporary viewing cache do not themselves mark it as saved offline.
_Avoid_: Device download, automatic offline availability.

**Guide download**:
A user-requested copy saved to the device's files through the download action. It is separate from opening the guide in the app or saving an app-managed offline copy.
_Avoid_: In-app viewing, temporary viewing cache.
