# Keep trips private and family content restricted by default

Although anyone can sign up, a trip remains private and joining requires an approved invitation. Within a trip, a family's checklist, bags, and guides stay accessible within that family unless explicitly shared with the whole trip. The user chose this boundary over making all family content visible to every trip member, so trip membership alone must not grant access to another family's records or files.

A trip organizer must also receive explicit permission from another family before reading or editing that family's private content. Managing trip details and invitations does not grant automatic access to family content.

Each family has at least one family coordinator who controls family membership, access grants, and sharing. The trip organizer controls trip details and trip invitations. A trip-shared bag may contain only trip-shared items, and adding a family-only item requires explicit sharing permission first.

Trip-shared content is readable by every trip member and editable by members with edit access. Only the owning family's coordinators can change its sharing permissions. Invitations target a specific verified email or existing username, with organizer approval for trip admission and coordinator approval for family access.
