# Keep trips private and family content restricted by default

Although anyone can sign up, a trip remains private and joining requires an approved invitation. Within a trip, a family's checklist, bags, and guides stay accessible to that family and trip organizers unless explicitly shared with the whole trip. The user chose this boundary over making all family content visible to every trip member, so trip membership alone must not grant access to another family's records or files.

Updated November 20, 2026: trip organizers can read, edit and manage every family and its content within their trip, including families created by another organizer. This replaces the earlier separate-family-grant requirement at the user's request. Organizer access is derived from current trip membership, so demotion removes it immediately; it does not leave permanent family grants. Members without organizer access still need explicit family permission.

Each family has at least one family coordinator who controls family membership, access grants, and sharing. The trip organizer controls trip details and trip invitations and can also manage family access. A trip-shared bag may contain only trip-shared items, and adding a family-only item requires explicit sharing permission first.

Trip-shared content is readable by every trip member and editable by members with edit access. The owning family's coordinators and trip organizers can change its sharing permissions. Invitations target a specific verified email or existing username, with organizer approval for trip admission and coordinator or trip-organizer approval for family access.
